# Privacy Policy for TeleportMe

**Effective Date:** July 18, 2026

**Crisnat** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, **TeleportMe** (the "App"), collects, uses, and discloses your information. 

By downloading, installing, or using TeleportMe, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

TeleportMe is designed primarily as a developer testing and privacy utility tool. We prioritize local data processing and minimal data collection.

### Location Data (Crucial Disclaimer)
To function, TeleportMe requires the following permissions:
*   `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`
*   `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`

**We DO NOT collect, harvest, transmit, or store your real or spoofed location data on any external servers.** 
The App utilizes these permissions exclusively to inject mock location coordinates into your device’s local operating system via the Android Developer Options (`Mock Location Provider`). All coordinate generation, route simulation, and location data processing happen entirely **locally on your device**.

### User-Provided Data
If you save favorite locations or custom routes within the App, this data is stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not have access to this data.

### Automatically Collected Data
We may use third-party tools (such as Google Play Services or Firebase Crashlytics) to monitor the app's performance and stability. This may include automatically collected, anonymous data such as:
*   Device type and model
*   Operating system version
*   App crash logs and diagnostic data
*   General usage telemetry (e.g., how often the app is opened)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Any anonymous diagnostic data collected is used solely to:
*   Identify and fix bugs, crashes, or performance issues.
*   Improve the user interface and overall experience of the App.
*   Serve relevant advertisements (if you are using the free, ad-supported version).
*   Process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

## 3. Foreground Services & Notifications

TeleportMe utilizes Android Foreground Services to maintain active mock location broadcasting while the App is running in the background. As mandated by Android OS policies, a persistent notification will be displayed while this service is active. This service does not transmit data off your device; it simply ensures the OS does not terminate the mock location process.

## 5. Security

We value your trust in providing us with your personal information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security. Since TeleportMe processes your core data locally, the risk of data breaches involving your location data through our services is effectively non-existent.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 18. If we discover that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers (note: we do not collect personal data to begin with). If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.
